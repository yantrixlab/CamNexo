# Security policy

## Known limitation

CamNexo does not yet pair devices or encrypt the connection. The video stream between the phone and
the computer travels in the clear on your local network, and the desktop receiver accepts a connection
from any device that can reach it. Use CamNexo only on networks you trust (for example your home or
office Wi-Fi) and not on public or shared Wi-Fi. Pairing with QR codes and an encrypted session are
planned, and this file will be updated when they ship.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Email **yantrixlab@gmail.com** with the
subject "Security", and include:

- what you found and where,
- steps to reproduce it,
- the version and operating system.

You will get an acknowledgement, and we will keep you updated while we fix it. Please give us a
reasonable time to release a fix before you share details publicly.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release receives fixes while the project is in early access.
